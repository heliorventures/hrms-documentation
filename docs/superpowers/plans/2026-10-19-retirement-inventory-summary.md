## Proposed cohesive implementation cuts and interfaces

1. **Reserve identifiers and protected destinations.** PostgreSQL candidate sequence starts at 0124+, subject to actual tenant/ops master inspection and coordinated reservations. ClickHouse already has migrations 0001/0002 and grants 0003/0004: never reuse those identities; reserve new unique IDs against its actual ledger. PostgreSQL production DDL/functions/grants stay in hrms-database, CH schema/grants in hrms-clickhouse and Kafka topics/ACL/connectors in hrms-kafka. Do not edit applied changesets or rename unrelated business APIs/configuration.
2. **Install authority and durable handoffs.** Ops authority/evidence precedes tenant handoffs, compact receipts and restartable exports. A typed tenant audit append port accepts tenant/actor/resource/action/time plus allowlisted payload and writes atomically, independently of optional organization membership. The private delivery producer writes pending payload plus immutable event/destination/dedupe/hash/producer-generation identity in the business transaction. Receivers acknowledge durable ownership or terminal outcome; hashes alone cannot support retries. Local delivery authority tracks lease/fence, attempts, next eligibility, accepted acknowledgement and bounded error code. Complete payloads/responses/attempts go to protected history.
3. **Move operational consumers/readers.** Replace survey operational audit reads with backfilled local transactional state, then preserve its management history API/UI contract. Preserve notification recipient/read version and occurrence uniqueness. Repoint offboarding to compact receipts. Switch every producer/worker/reader/export dependency; fence old producer and worker generations before destination side effects. Preserve business scheduler work when dismantling legacy worker dispatch.
4. **Export and reconcile before destruction.** Full raw archives identify source DB/schema/table, tenant and original UUID; retain every original column/type/timestamp and canonical row hash. Establish finite commit-safe cutoffs under a producer fence; use restartable acknowledged chunks and conflicting-identity rejection. Preserve scoped historical access after detachment. Verify source-ID/hash parity, protected reads, restore and accepted ownership of each pending/in-flight/retry event. A zero backlog or transformed export COMPLETE is insufficient.
5. **Gate exact retirement.** Require fresh reviewed evidence bound to cutoff, source generation/current authority, partition barriers, destination acknowledgements and archive/delivery digests. Lock authority/source while checking. Missing/expired/revoked/mismatched evidence, gaps/quarantine, stale binaries or unexpected dependencies halt without deletion. Repoint FKs and explicitly remove obsolete routines/triggers, retire exact tables without CASCADE, preserve compact receipts/cutover/restore evidence. Pre-destruction rollback uses accepted generation rollback; post-destruction recovery restores verified full archives.

## Delivery report integration and capture design constraint

Source: `2026-10-19-retirement-delivery-inventory.md` (verified static findings). Existing Kafka consumers are inbox/history/reporting only; no external dispatch consumer exists, and current exports cover neither legacy delivery table nor private Finance delivery. The replacement must create durable per-destination jobs in its own consumer group before acknowledging offsets, with immutable source/tenant/event/destination/contract identity, hash-conflict rejection and generation-fenced retry updates. Historical replay must never authorize a new side effect.

**Top-level capture challenge:** Current guards reject unrelated publications because PostgreSQL lacks publication ACLs. A new private publication on the same database breaks that boundary; granting another CDC role does not repair it. The capture design remains unresolved: preserve a safe single authorized publication/table boundary with separately routed protected encrypted payloads, or publish references resolved only by a source-bound authenticated adapter. Either option requires an explicit revised contract, topic/consumer isolation and proof that reporting principals cannot recover private payloads. Do not add raw delivery payloads to the existing company/reporting topic or casually relax the all-publications guard.

Existing webhook envelope is schema v1 with original outbox_id, tenant/aggregate/type/payload/time. Active subscriptions match event names case-insensitively or wildcard; an optional platform destination adds fanout. Any 2xx currently counts as success. One aggregate status covers all destinations, missing destinations can mark PROCESSED, partial fanout retries successful destinations, and processing has no durable retry delay or claim-generation completion fence. Preserve original IDs; separate no-destination/skipped/failed/delivered/uncertain outcomes. Historical destination sets and receiver dedupe/receipt contracts need operational reconciliation. Logs lack a dedicated outbox ID; correlate only a verified stored envelope, retaining uncorrelatable rows as history.

A verified offboarding producer/allowlist mismatch excludes `separation` from current claims and analytics. Define its entitlement policy explicitly and include excluded pending rows in migration parity. Replacement GraphQL inspection/requeue and UI module-health/probe callers must preserve scope and permissions.

Keep the existing private Employee→Payroll protocol: only operation references and typed actions cross the private stream; a source adapter signs a fresh request per attempt and validates signed tenant/request/action/expected-state responses. Delayed signed envelopes expire. Preserve authoritative Employee OperationStatus readback, authorization binding, loan conflict holds, Payroll's transactional FNF creation and independent reconciliation worker. No service secrets, client JWTs, arbitrary DB credentials or caller-selected destinations enter shared delivery.

Before removing the old polling outbox worker binary, rehost all source-local jobs independently of delivery: due offboarding/access/session revocation, attendance expiry, survey lifecycle, birthday/anniversary privacy and occurrence dedupe, performance cycle generation/stage deadlines, staged company-file and announcement-video expiry, and private-file cleanup. Preserve bounded sweeps, tenant clock/entitlement gates and retry semantics. Survey receipts/state remain compact local business authority. No replacement legacy outbox polling worker is intended.

Required delivery security corrections accompany the new adapter contract: destination egress policy with DNS/redirect protections, bounded request/response reads and character-safe diagnostics, per-destination signing credentials/rotation, malformed-key rejection and redaction. Current source does not demonstrate SSRF safety or per-subscription outbound signing. Verify timeout/ack loss, partial fanout, expired claim overlap, retry caps/DLQ/replay, absent destinations, 429/5xx/4xx classification and receiver idempotency in staging before declaring delivery complete.
