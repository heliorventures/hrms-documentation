## Security contracts reviewers must trace to actual code

1. Separate company databases; deployment-bound endpoints/topics/storage/credentials. Browser-selected URLs, shared arbitrary SQL/GraphQL forwarding and fabricated source users are forbidden. Organization management alone never grants employee/account/payroll authority.
2. Every business command binds the exact actor, organization/company/membership, operation/resource/version, explicit companywide capability, current identity/link/grant epochs and original body hash. Source domain validation, target-account/self guards, eligible last administrator, local transaction locks and original principal attribution must survive alternate callers and revocation.
3. Employee login provisioning uses the real onboarding transaction and `role:manage`; no arbitrary account relink. Passwords are request-only, cleared on submit and absent from payload journal/replay, browser storage, history and logs. Recovery offers reference/status/cancel only for credential-bearing commands.
4. Payroll ACCEPTED is queue acceptance, never business completion. No reusable tokens persisted. Current cryptographically verified authority is refreshed at execution; job lease and business effects share transaction/commit fencing. Restart/replay cannot duplicate business execution; expired/stale leases cannot commit; only queued jobs can cancel.
5. Inbox/history/reporting have independent groups/checkpoints and sink acknowledgements. Health requires source LSN/capture confirmation, persisted Connect offsets, accepted bootstrap and every expected partition/role barrier for the current epoch. Quarantine stays incomplete; unknown quantities do not become zero or invented history.
6. History export uses genuine source provenance, immutable IDs/chunk/range/hash continuity and broker ACK before persisted publish progress. Archive requests bind tenant/path/body/time and fixed trust; authenticated encrypted manifests preserve exact envelopes. Central checks current `history:read=ALL` and `history:export=ALL` before/after I/O and exact identity/range/count/field output. Historical accepted archive epochs remain immutable.
7. Retention defaults dry-run; no business/receipt/audit deletion. Execution requires trusted policy, exact source scope, fresh capture/history/restore/cutover evidence, locked candidate IDs/hashes/ranges and final expiry rechecks. Existing production data and deployment remain release operations.
8. UI keeps authentication tenant as the login company; target membership is distinct. Pending/cancellation uncertainty retains source references; only matching committed receipts report business success. Fresh access/source reads follow authority changes; failed/incomplete pagination cannot silently clear intent.
