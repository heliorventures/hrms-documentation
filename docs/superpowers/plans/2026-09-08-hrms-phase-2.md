- [ ] Add tenant/designation/employee policy configuration, independently optional monthly/yearly/unused-balance limits, validity days, claim deadline and approved-cancellation setting. Policy is disabled until configured. Preserve employee override priority.
- [ ] Submit half/full-day claims for any worked date, with employee identity from the session. Manager/HR permission and target scope govern decisions; no self approval. Serialize employee claim/credit changes and prevent credit duplication and more than one full-day credit for a worked date.

Reserve earliest-expiring eligible credits; all leave dates must precede credit expiry. Expired/released allocations cannot extend credit expiry or create spendable expired credit.
